# Reference Report — CPP Skeletal-Maturation Study (TW3 / SMI / CVMI)

**Type:** External evidence / clarification report · **Created:** 2026-07-13
**Purpose:** Read the supplied study data precisely, and state — honestly — what it can and cannot do for the product's TW3 accuracy. This document is a *reference artifact*: Plan 2 (and later plans) cite it where the data legitimately informs design.
**Source status:** ⚠️ **To be confirmed.** The tables are consistent with a published CPP-vs-control cephalometric study (styling/design typical of a Korean orthodontic/pediatric journal). The exact citation, year, and **study population** are not established from the excerpt and must be recorded before this data is used as a reference anywhere patient-facing.

---

## 1. What this dataset is (one paragraph)

A cross-sectional comparison of **central precocious puberty (CPP, n = 70)** vs **control (n = 48)** children, all around 8 years chronological age, measured on three *different* skeletal-maturity systems — **TW3 hand-wrist bone age**, **SMI (Fishman skeletal maturity indicator, hand-wrist)**, and **CVMI (cervical vertebral maturation indicator, lateral cephalogram)** — plus a set of craniofacial cephalometric dimensions. The headline finding is that **hand-wrist skeletal maturity (TW3 and SMI) is significantly advanced in CPP and discriminates CPP from controls, whereas cervical maturation (CVMI) does not** at this age.

**Relevance to TW3:** **Tangential.** This concerns craniofacial growth, not hand-wrist skeletal scoring. It supports the general thesis "somatic/skeletal maturation is advanced in CPP" but does **not** touch the bone-age engine. Recorded for completeness; not used by Plan 2.

## 3. What this data **can** do for the product (honest)

1. **Serve as reference/context evidence.** It documents, from an external cohort, the expected direction and rough magnitude of skeletal-age advancement in CPP (bone age running ~1–2 yr ahead of chronological age; modal TW3 ~9–10 yr at chronological ~8 yr). Useful for interpretation copy and clinician context — *labelled as external, population-to-confirm*.
2. **Provide plausibility priors for monitoring (Plan 2).** As the flywheel accumulates clinician-confirmed bone ages, we can sanity-check the *shape* of our own CPP-indication distribution and the bone-age−chronological-age gap against a published cohort. This is a **soft plausibility signal**, never a hard gate (population differs; see §4).
3. **Justify SMI as an optional secondary hand-wrist cross-check, and de-prioritize CVMI.** The data shows SMI discriminates and correlates moderately; CVMI is weak at these ages. If the clinic ever records these, SMI is the more informative secondary signal — but as a **separate method, never averaged into TW3** (same rule the codebase already enforces for the whole-image AI cross-check).

## 4. What this data **cannot** do (equally important)

1. **It does not make the TW3 engine more accurate on its own.** Engine accuracy is gated on: the licensed TW3 point tables + score→age curves (**Plan 1**), per-bone labelled training data (**Plan 2 → Plan 4**), and an external validation cohort (**Plan 5**). This study provides **none** of those. It is summary statistics, not images, not per-bone stages, not the copyrighted tables.
2. **It is aggregate, not per-patient.** There are no individual rows to learn from or validate against — only group means and band counts. You cannot compute a per-image error or calibrate a curve from it.
3. **SMI ≠ TW3, and CVMI is a different modality.** SMI is Fishman's 11-stage (here 1–7) hand-wrist system; CVMI is cervical-vertebra staging from a *lateral cephalogram*. Neither is interchangeable with TW3, and neither may be blended into a TW3 bone age.
4. **Population is unconfirmed and probably not Indian.** The product is India-calibrated (IAP/Khadilkar). If this cohort is (as the styling suggests) East Asian, its advancement magnitudes are **context, not a target** for Indian children. It cannot serve as the Indian validation cohort Plan 5 needs.

**Bottom line:** valuable as **reference evidence and monitoring priors**, not as calibration or training data. Using it as more than that would repeat exactly the kind of overclaim the master audit was written to prevent.

## 6. Open questions to confirm (before any citation/use)

1. **Full citation + year + journal**, and — critically — the **study population** (country/ethnicity).
2. **What is Table E's correlation *with*?** (TW3 skeletal age? chronological age?) The rank order is usable now; the correlate must be confirmed before it's quoted.
3. **SMI staging system** in use (Fishman 11-stage collapsed to 1–7 here?) and **CVMI system** (which CVM scale, collapsed to 1–3?).
4. **CPP definition + treatment status** at imaging (untreated vs on GnRH analogue) — this changes how the advancement magnitude should be read.
5. Whether the clinic can obtain the **underlying per-patient data** — only that would upgrade this from "reference" to "validation-relevant."
